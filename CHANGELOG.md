# Release notes

Every released version of ScrappWars, newest first. Downloads: [releases](https://github.com/RobertAgterhuis/Game-ScrappWars/releases).

## 1.1.0 fix 1 (1.1.0.1, 2026-11-04)

Fix 1 for 1.1: the first stretch of More of Kessra. The open desert between the regions gets more places to charge, more places to stop and look, and more life on its riverbeds and ridges.

- **More charging spots.** Eight new solar masts stand beside the long routes, so no stretch of road runs much more than 75 m from a place to charge.
- **Five new lookouts** on the sandstone ridges between the regions: over the Scorch, the Crater Sea, the Dawnbreaker, the east ridge and the way to Hollowmere. Rest at one and the camera eases out over the view, and the map gets charted round the spot and towards what you are looking at.
- **Riverbeds and ridges come alive.** Scrub, tufts and stones grow on the floors of the dry riverbeds and in the lee of the ridges, and solid fallen boulders lie in the scree at the ridges' feet. The roads themselves stay clear.
- **Behind the scenes:** the automatic bug sweep no longer mistakes a spinning part crate for a changed area.

Saves, settings, photos and Scrap Marks carry over.

## 1.1.0 (1.1.0.0, 2026-11-04)

The "Ceasefire" update: the whole of ScrappWars is out for everyone, from the crash of the Patient Wren to the day the relief ship lands, with the bugs the beta turned up fixed. This is version 1.1: it brings the 1.0 beta to every player, together with all its fixes, so stable players and beta players move to the same game.

### What it brings

Everything the 1.0 beta had, now on the stable channel:

- **A new look for Kessra.** Re-keyed light for every hour, reshaped wrecks and rocks, new ground and mineral materials, rebuilt effects and a real Ultra setting.
- **Clouds over the desert**, dusk cirrus, and Vashka in colour by day.
- **Controller vibration** for hits, the EMP, the thruster, the grapple and the magnet legs, and for how SD-7 moves: landings, footsteps and rolling over sand, metal and rock. Two sliders on the Controls page set the strength.
- **Credits**, from the main menu and from the ending screen.

### Solid where it looks solid

During the beta an automatic sweep drove SD-7 into every object in the world on every set of legs and swung the camera round it. More than 400 places it found are fixed:

- **No more falling out of the world** or getting stuck for good: in Canyon City's cliff homes, at the supply vaults, in the Scorch, among the field drums and the Salt Graveyard's ribs.
- **Wrecks, ribs, rocks and ships are solid as they look**: the Scorch's war machines, the Rib Field, the buttes and their scree, the Seedbearer, the Salt Graveyard's ships, the container field and its crane, the escape pods and the Patient Wren's hull and hold.
- **SD-7 no longer drives through or sinks into** loader scrappers, survey drones, memory pedestals, rescue beacons, charge pads, power cells, crates, tarps, relays, beacons, trees, camps, ramps, leaning plates, Canyon City's houses and lifts, Dunewell's well, awnings and dew traps, the mine's arches and rails, and the bones in the desert.
- **Magnet legs** only grip faces wide enough to stand on, never climb plaster, tents or rock, and never flip onto the far side of a thin part.
- **The camera** keeps its eye on the line it has checked, so it no longer slips into overhangs, canopies and ceilings above SD-7.
- Small gaps where only a jump got SD-7 out are closed or widened.

### Your saves

Saves, settings, photos and Scrap Marks carry over. Beta players can turn **Include beta versions** off again under Settings, Updates; this release is newer than the beta, so nobody has to go back.

## 1.0.1 beta fix 1 (1.0.1.1, 2026-11-02)

Beta fix 1 for 1.0: groundwork for hunting down the bugs where SD-7 sinks into things or the camera ends up inside them.

- **Better bug reports from your own play.** When the game has to catch SD-7 after it falls through the world, or the camera ends up inside an object, the local session log now notes where it happened, in which area and on which legs. Attach your session log when you report a bug (Ctrl+Alt+B) and we can go straight to the spot.

## 1.0.1 beta (1.0.1.0, 2026-11-02)

The "Ceasefire" update: ScrappWars 1.0 is in beta. The whole game, from the crash of the Patient Wren to the day the relief ship lands, is in and complete; this beta is for finding the bugs that are left before 1.0 comes out for everyone.

### How to join

- In the main menu, open Settings, then Updates, and turn on **Include beta versions**. The game offers the beta the next time it checks for updates.
- Without it, you stay on the stable version (0.12) until 1.0 is released for everyone.
- Your saves, settings and photos carry over. You can go back to stable at any time from the same page, once a stable version newer than your beta is out.

### What 1.0 brings

Everything from the "Hard Light" update and its seven fixes is in the beta:

- **A new look for Kessra.** Re-keyed light for every hour, reshaped wrecks and rocks, new ground and mineral materials, rebuilt effects and a real Ultra setting.
- **Clouds over the desert**, dusk cirrus, and Vashka in colour by day.
- **Controller vibration** for hits, the EMP, the thruster, the grapple and the magnet legs, and for how SD-7 moves: landings, footsteps and rolling over sand, metal and rock. Two sliders on the Controls page set the strength.
- **Credits**, from the main menu and from the ending screen.
- Many smaller fixes: the Long Night's skyline, SD-7's paint seen from the front, the way into the Workshop, falling through the world, sand you could drive through, and more.

### Help us find what is left

Press Ctrl+Alt+B wherever something goes wrong. The game takes a screenshot and opens a filled-in bug report on GitHub. Everything you report before 1.0 is fixed in a beta update first.

## 0.12.0 fix 6 (0.12.0.6, 2026-11-01)

Fix 6 for "Hard Light": you feel how SD-7 moves.

- **Landing.** Coming down after a jump or a drop now gives a thump on the controller. A small hop is a faint tick; a jump off a rock or a wreck lands hard.
- **Footsteps.** On spider legs or magnet legs every footfall gives a light tick, a little crisper on metal than on sand.
- **Rolling.** On wheels the controller rumbles softly with your speed and with the ground under you: smooth on sand, rattling on a metal hull, bumpy over rock and glass. Standing still, flying and hovering stay quiet.
- **Movement rumble.** A new slider on the Controls page, under "Controller vibration", sets how strong movement feels or turns it off. Hits, the EMP and the other big moments keep their own rumble.

## 0.12.0 fix 5 (0.12.0.5, 2026-11-01)

Fix 5 for "Hard Light": the controller rumbles.

- **Controller vibration.** When you play with a controller, it now rumbles with the game: bites, a part breaking, the EMP, thruster bursts, the grapple's claw latching, the magnet legs gripping a hull, the cutting torch humming as it burns, the sand worm and heavy impacts, the Patient Wren's crash in the intro and the relief lander touching down.
- **Its own setting.** "Controller vibration" on the Controls page sets the strength or turns it off. It is separate from screen shake, so you can keep one without the other.
- **Only when you use the controller.** It stops at once when you pause, switch to another window or pick up the keyboard.

## 0.12.0 (0.12.0.0, 2026-10-31)

The "Hard Light" update: Kessra gets a new look. The light has been re-keyed for every hour, the great wrecks and rocks have new shapes, the ground and minerals have new materials, and the effects have been rebuilt, with Ultra now a real top setting of its own.

### Light and sky

- **Dawn and dusk are no longer a pink wash.** The sky cools away from the suns, a warm band hugs the horizon on their side, and shadows go properly dark. Both sun discs read at a binary sunset.
- **The double shadow reads.** The major sun's shadow is filled cool by the minor sun, the minor's warm by the major.
- **Noon has contrast.** Shadows are about twice as deep and the sky is blue, never brighter than the sand.
- **Night is moonlit, not mauve.** Mote's lamp now waits for a low sun instead of warming the dusk.
- **Vashka and Teru are lit as spheres.** Vashka has curving cloud bands and an atmosphere rim, and Teru's rings cast and catch shadow. Both sit behind clouds and the horizon where they should.

### Wrecks and machines

- **The Dawnbreaker reads as the battleship from the intro:** a chined hull with an armour belt and casemates, a broken-off wedge prow and a stepped pagoda tower.
- **The Long Night** carries a scavenged crane, strapped pods and open engine blocks.
- **The Patient Wren** has her bridge tower, funnel, mast and cargo gantry back. Her hold is now a real interior with water tanks, seed racks and the signal-core cradle.
- **The escape pod at home** is a scorched re-entry capsule with a heat shield, retro rockets and a blown parachute canister.
- **Mote, the Rib Field and the spider legs:** Mote has a dented, lived-in body. The Rib Field's walker frames are bowed ribs hung with torn plating. The spider legs are load-bearing limbs with knees and claws.
- **SD-7's paint shows.** Every livery now reads on the drum from the game camera.

### The land

- **Buttes, mesas and arches** are eroded rock with caprock, collapse scars and talus instead of round drums.
- **Salt and crystal:**
  - Salt pillars are stepped halite crags.
  - The salt flats are a cracked crust.
  - Obsidian spires are smoky black glass.
  - Cave crystals glow from inside a clear shell.
- **The ground:**
  - The low-sun blotches in the sand are gone.
  - Canyon rims and table-lands no longer show sawteeth.
  - Gullies have cracked clay.
  - Hollowmere's floor is mud.
- **The Crater Sea's rim** has slumps, gullies and drifted sand.
- **Hollowmere's mangroves** stand on arching prop roots, with real vines and water lilies on a reflective swamp.
- **Dunewell** has new wind-catcher heads, and the traders' camps are fully kitted out.

### Effects

- **One effects language.** Dust, smoke and sparks share lit, soft materials across the game.
- **Scanner, EMP and lightning:**
  - The scanner sweep is a soft phosphor curtain.
  - The EMP is a pressure shell with a sand ring, no longer a glass dome.
  - Glass Plain lightning branches and arcs from spire to spire.
- **The thruster** throws a small warm pool and a sand wash.
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - Scan labels stay out of photos.
  - Thin poles between the camera and SD-7 fade.
  - The ending's beacon columns stay out of the relief arrival.
- **Scratches only where metal is touched,** no more white scratch overlay on whole walls.

### Ultra and performance

- **Ultra is its own tier:** soft sun and moon shadows and finer reflections. High, Medium and Low keep their cost.
- **Cheaper shadows** in Dunewell and on the open ground, and scavengers that draw about five times cheaper.
- **The title screen draws a third of what it did.**
- **The automatic graphics choice is recalibrated,** so slower cards no longer start on Ultra.

### New CC0 assets

Rock, salt, clay, mud and bark scans and eight salvage props from Poly Haven, ambientCG and BlendKit, all public domain (CC0) and listed in the game's third-party notes.

### Fixes

- Cave scavenger packs spawned under the ground; they now start on the cave floor.
- The rescue beacon and charge pad lamps no longer burn at full by day.

## 0.11.0 (0.11.0.0, 2026-10-31)

The "Three Days Later" update: WREN now notices what SD-7 has already done, the story tells one consistent account from the intro to the last log, and the ending goes on to the day the first relief ship lands.

### WREN notices

- **Ahead of her? She says so.** Find a part, reach a place or finish a job before WREN asks, and she no longer briefs you on something you have already done. She tells you she noticed, and why it mattered: why the transmitter needs that piece, why the spider legs, what the welded bulkhead is for.
- **Side jobs you already finished** are acknowledged and paid at once instead of being handed out as new work.
- **She remembers who you have met.** Once you have read the memory a lamp, a coupler or a deflector belongs to, WREN can name the person who left it behind.
- **Loading a save stays quiet.** Jobs that opened while you were away arrive as one note in the journal.

### One story

- **One account of the night the Patient Wren fell.** Each fleet was told a different lie about her on the same night, and every log, intro line, townsfolk remark and WREN line now tells it the same way.
- **Directions and timeline fixed.** Where the Dawnbreaker came down, where the Scorch lies, which way Hollowmere is and when the Wren fell now agree with the map and with each other. The fleets' colours and the Wren's own colours match what you see.
- **Re-voiced lines.** WREN has more than fifty new and re-recorded lines, and several crew memories and one intro line were recorded again.
- **A miner's lamp** now lies in the caves beside the crystal WREN talks about.

### Pacing

- **Side jobs are spread out** along the main line instead of five arriving at once.
- **WREN's remarks no longer arrive late.** A comment on a storm or a part waits for her to finish talking, and one that would come too late is dropped instead of playing out of context.
- **When several things are done early at once,** WREN keeps it short.
- **The Dawnbreaker's deck relays wake when WREN asks for them.** Before that they are dead, so the ship stays calm until the last climb begins. A save that already powered the bridge keeps its power.

### A new ending scene

- **"Three days later."** After the epilogue the first relief ship comes down beside the escape pod in the binary morning, lowers its ramp, and its crew walk out to meet SD-7. It plays in the game's own light, with your scrapper's own parts, paint and wear, and can be skipped like the other cinematics.

### Fixes

- **The day and night clock runs again after the ending.** Keep exploring and the sun moves on from where it was, instead of staying stuck at the epilogue's sunset.
- **Old saves and the torch.** A save from before the torch moved earlier in the story is sent back to pick it up, then continues where it was.
- **Scans find later targets.** A scanner pulse now locates any upcoming target in range, not only the current one.
- A finished story no longer briefs objectives that were still open when the cease-fire came.
- WREN no longer repeats her current request after you finish a side job, and long "you were ahead of me" runs are kept short without losing the important beats.

## 0.10.0 (0.10.0.0, 2026-10-30)

The "Scrap Marks" update: SD-7 now collects stamped steel tags for what it does on Kessra, earns medals for full sets and climbs a service rank. Everything you earn is for show; SD-7 plays exactly as before.

### Scrap Marks

- **60 marks** in eight sets: Story, Exploration, Salvage & rescue, Survival, Craft, Trade, Photography and a few hidden ones. Each is a small steel tag with a punched symbol and a serial number.
- **Earned in play only.** A mark counts the moment it happens. Loading a save never hands out marks, and every mark outside the story can still be earned in an existing save; story marks come with a new game.
- **Shared by your slots.** Marks are kept in your profile, so all three save slots add to the same record. The Load view shows each slot's marks.
- **Eight medals** on canvas ribbons, one for every complete set, and a **service rank** from Class D up to "Last Scrapper".
- **A stamp notice** comes in at the top right and waits for storms, cinematics, photo mode and WREN. Settings > Scrap Mark notices: stamp and sound, silent, or off.
- **The journal has a MARKS page,** and the main menu has a **Service record**. Switch the journal's pages with left and right or the shoulder buttons.

### Rewards, all cosmetic

- Two new paint jobs: Wren hull grey and Bare metal.
- A survey grid and your rank stencilled on SD-7's back plate (you can switch the stencil off).
- The Storm print photo look and the Survey plate photo frame.
- Seedling Mote, with a little planter on its back.
- Dunewell's people start treating you as a regular, and WREN has a commendation for you.
- **The trophy wall at home is now a service board:** medals on their ribbons, every tag on its own peg with an outline for the ones still to find, your rank on the header and a lamp at night. A chain of tags hangs in the escape pod too.

### New things to do in finished places

- Listen to a repaired beacon, pick over a downed scrap hunter, look through an empty locker, check the home systems or wipe a solar mirror. Gauge in Dunewell tells old stories once every memory is found.

### Fixes

- **No more freeze after loading or swapping a part.** Since 0.9 the game could stall for a second, and on some machines freeze for good, right after a game loaded, after a part swap or when scavengers appeared. The shadow work behind it now runs without blocking the rest of the game.

## 0.9.0 (0.9.0.0, 2026-10-30)

The "clear the backlog" update: you choose how you play and where your game is kept, SD-7 walks and looks better than ever, and the big wrecks, the light and the frame rate all got a pass. Everything plays as before.

### Your game, your way

- **Choose your controller:** Settings > Controls now lets you pick Automatic, Keyboard & mouse or Game controller. Automatic follows whatever you touched last; an explicit choice plays with that device only, and the other one can never lock you out.
- **Rebind almost every action:** any action can be moved to another key, mouse button, controller button, trigger or stick direction; only the pause menu and the right-stick camera stay where they are. If the input is already taken, the game offers to swap the two. Each device can be reset on its own.
- **A live diagram** of the keyboard and mouse or the controller shows what every key and button does, and lights up as you press it; its labels never cross each other.
- **Every prompt follows your device:** hints, the map, photo mode and WREN's subtitles name the key or button you actually use.
- **Choose where your game is kept:** Settings > Data folder moves your saves, settings, photos and reports to a folder of your own. Every file is checked after the copy, nothing is ever overwritten, and if that folder goes missing (an unplugged drive), the game says so, saves in the default folder for that session and goes back to your folder once it is there again.
- **A fitting first graphics setting:** on its first start the game looks at your graphics card and picks a tier that runs well. A setting you chose yourself is never changed.

- **Real knees:** the spider legs fold at the knee and keep their feet planted on steps and slopes, also when limping.
- **The iris works:** SD-7's big lens opens in the dark and stops down in bright sunlight, step by step.

## 0.8.0 (0.8.0.0, 2026-10-30)

The "Twin Sunset" update: every machine, wreck, creature and corner of Kessra has been rebuilt to look harder used, patched together and lived in, and the desert now ends every day with two suns going down together. Everything plays exactly as before.

### Two suns

- **Binary sunsets:** Kessra's small, pale-blue second sun now travels beside the big one, a little lower, and sets with it. At dawn and dusk SD-7 throws two overlapping shadows (on High and Ultra; on Ultra the big wrecks and rocks do too).
- **Vashka** hangs larger in the sky in soft cream and orange bands, and glows faintly at night.
- **Salt and heat:** salt pans crust the low ground of the open desert, the far horizon boils with heat shimmer and mirage, and a sandstorm tears the clouds apart into an ochre haze.

### SD-7 and its parts

- **A new face:** SD-7 looks out through one big round camera lens that focuses in little motor steps, hunts when you scan and goes still when it runs flat.
- **A jury-rigged body:** dented plates, hand-laid welds, bare battery leads, a rusty dish on its back. When a part is close to breaking, SD-7 looks it: a cover plate hangs off its last bolt, the lens cracks, the worst part smokes and the leads spark. A repair cleans it up again.
- **Parts that work:** every upgrade is a worn machine you can watch at work. Magnet pads spark, spider legs hiss, the thruster coughs dirty flame, the torch tip glows and spits, the grapple's claw flies out on a cable and winches back, the scanner's lenses click, blaster bolts fly lit across the sand and the shield ripples amber where it is hit.
- **Feet on the ground:** SD-7 tilts with the slope, its wheels roll true and its feet plant, a broken leg limps visibly, and it leaves tracks in the sand that the wind fills in.
- **Wear that stays put:** chips, rust and burns now stay on moving machines instead of sliding over them.

### The people and machines of Kessra

- **WREN's radio** squelches open and shut, crackles and distorts on a weak signal, and her panel is a green phosphor scope.
- **Mote** is a dented little box with one amber eye on a sputtering lift field.
- **Dunewell's mechanics** wear heavy robes, masks, goggles and greasy tool belts. The stranded scrappers lie buried to their shoulders in the sand.
- **Scavengers** click their pincers and rear back hissing from your headlamp; **scrap hunters** are lean, dog-like hunters whose twin sensors follow you; the sand worm ploughs with a heavy, plated wedge of a snout.
- **Rival fleets:** the Directorate's wrecks are hard-edged slate and navy with brass trim and bare bellies; the Sable fleet's are salvage, mismatched plates bolted on and canvas strapped down for roofs.

### Places

- **Home:** the escape pod's hatch is now a ramp, and you can drive inside its cramped cabin with hammocks, terminals and a workbench full of loose tools.
- **Dunewell** has grown into a dense town of mud domes, every house with a turning wind-catcher, and the stalls hang with coarse burlap.
- **Canyon City** climbs its deep orange walls: homes and rooms carved into the rock, clanking chain lifts and an iron foundry smoking on the summit.
- **The Dawnbreaker** has a collapsed corridor in its torn flank, wiring dangling in the wind and sand streaming through; the **Patient Wren** lies broken in half with her machinery spilled into the dunes; the **Seedbearer** breathes thick white mist over Hollowmere's vine-choked mangroves.

## 0.5.0 fix 1 (0.5.0.1, 2026-10-29)

### New

- **Report a bug from inside the game.** Press Ctrl+Alt+B wherever something
  goes wrong. The game saves a screenshot, pauses and asks what happened. "Open
  on GitHub" opens a new issue in your browser, already filled in with your text
  and the game's version, place and settings (optionally the last lines of the
  log, without paths or your user name), and shows the screenshot in its folder
  so you can drag it in. You send the issue with your own GitHub account; the
  game never posts anything by itself.
